1. Features
Add and view sports equipment.
Search equipment by name.
Track available stock.
Rent equipment to customers.
Record equipment returns.
Prevent rentals when stock is unavailable.
Track rental due dates.
Calculate rental charges.
View overdue rentals.
Generate stock and rental reports.
